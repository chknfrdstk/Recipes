# Honey Garlic Pork Tenderloin

## Ingredients

- 1 lb pork tenderloin
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds (optional)
- 2 green onions, sliced (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F
2. In a small bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, minced garlic, olive oil, ground ginger, and black pepper until well combined.
3. Place the pork tenderloin in baking dish and pour the honey garlic mixture over the top, ensuring it's well coated.
4. Bake in the preheated oven for 25-30 minutes, or until the pork reaches an internal temperature of 145°F.
5. Baste the pork with the sauce halfway through cooking for extra flavor.
6. Once done, remove from the oven and let it rest for 5 minutes before slicing.
7. Drizzle the remaining sauce from the baking dish over the sliced pork, and sprinkle with sesame seeds and sliced green onions for garnish.
