# Slow Cooker Thai Peanut Pork

## Ingredients

- 2 lb pork shoulder or pork loin
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp sriracha sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 1/4 cup chopped peanuts (for garnish)
- 1 tbsp chopped cilantro (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Place the pork shoulder in the slow cooker.
2. In a bowl, mix together peanut butter, soy sauce, rice vinegar, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, sriracha sauce (if using), and water.
3. Pour the peanut sauce over the pork in the slow cooker.
4. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours, or until the pork is tender and easily shreds with a fork.
5. Once done, shred the pork using two forks and stir it into the sauce.
6. Serve the pork over rice, noodles, or with steamed vegetables. Garnish with chopped peanuts and cilantro.
