# Jerk Pork, Pineapple Salsa, and Coconut Rice Bowls with Caribbean Aioli

## Ingredients

### For the Pork

- 1 lb pork chops, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp jerk seasoning

### For the Bowls

- 1 cup cooked coconut rice
- 1 cup diced pineapple
- 1/4 cup chopped red onion
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1/4 cup chopped jalapeño (optional)

### For the Caribbean Aioli

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine pork with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and jerk seasoning.
2. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
3. Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Grill the pork for 2-3 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
4. In a bowl, combine pineapple, red onion, cilantro, and jalapeño (if using).
5. In a small bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, Greek yogurt, garlic, lime juice, salt, pepper, and cumin.
6. Divide the coconut rice among bowls. Top with grilled pork, pineapple salsa, and drizzle generously with the Caribbean Aioli.

## Tips

For a spicier sauce, add more jalapeño to the Pineapple Salsa or a pinch of red pepper flakes to the Caribbean Aioli.
