# Fiery Shrimp Fajita Bowl with Cilantro Lime Crema

## Ingredients

- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika

### For the Fajita Vegetables

- 1 bell pepper (any color), sliced
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Cilantro Lime Crema

- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine shrimp with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, chili powder, and paprika. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
2. Preheat skillet to medium-high heat.
3. Cook the shrimp for 2-3 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
4. To the same skillet add the bell pepper and onion slices until softened and slightly charred.
5. In a small bowl, whisk together Greek yogurt, cilantro, lime juice, garlic, salt, and pepper.
6. Divide the fajita vegetables among bowls. Top with grilled shrimp and drizzle generouslv with the Cilantro Lime Crema.
