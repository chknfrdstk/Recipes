# Hawaiian Shrimp Bowl with Sriracha Mayo

## Ingredients

### For the Shrimp

- 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

### For the Bowl

- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1/4 cup chopped green onions
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro

### For the Sriracha Mayo

- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/4 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/8 tsp garlic powder

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine shrimp with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
2. Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Grill the shrimp for 2-3 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, sriracha, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and garlic powder.
4. Divide the rice among bowls. Top with grilled shrimp, green onions, and cilantro.
5. Drizzle generously with the Sriracha Mayo.

## Tips

- For a spicier sauce, add more sriracha or a pinch of red pepper flakes to the Sriracha Mayo.
- Serve the bowls with your favorite side dishes, such as seaweed salad, pickled ginger, or edamame.
