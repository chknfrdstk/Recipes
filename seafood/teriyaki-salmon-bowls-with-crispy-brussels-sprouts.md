# Teriyaki Salmon Bowls with Crispy Brussels Sprouts

## Ingredients

### For the Salmon

- 1 lb salmon fillets
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

### For the Brussels Sprouts

- 1 pound Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

### For the Sauce

- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Marinade

In a small bowl, combine soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, ginger, garlic powder, and red pepper flakes. Place salmon in a shallow dish and pour the marinade over it. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.

### 2. Prepare the Brussels Sprouts

Toss Brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Grill or broil salmon and Brussels sprouts for 5-7 minutes per side, or until cooked through.

### 3. Make the Sauce

In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, honey, lemon juice, and garlic powder. Mix well.

### 4. Assemble the Bowls

Serve salmon and Brussels sprouts over rice or quinoa. Drizzle with sauce.

## Tips

- For a spicier dish, add more red pepper flakes to the marinade.
- You can use different types of fish, such as halibut or cod.
