# Easy Ginger Soy Cod

## Ingredients

- 4 cod fillets (about 5-6 oz each)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil (or sesame oil for extra flavor)
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or lime juice)
- 1 tbsp freshly grated ginger
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp cornstarch (optional, for thickening sauce
- 2 green onions, sliced (for garnish)
- 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds (optional, for garnish)
- Cooked white or jasmine rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Pat cod fillets dry with paper towels. Lightly season both sides with salt and pepper.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, grated ginger, garlic, and cornstarch (if using). Set aside.
3. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add cod fillets and sear for about 3-4 minutes on one side until golden brown. Flip and cook another 3 minutes, or until the fish flakes easily with a fork.
4. Reduce heat to low. Pour in the ginger soy mixture. Spoon the sauce over the fish as it simmers and thickens slightly (1-2 minutes). Remove from heat.
5. Plate the cod over hot rice. Drizzle with extra sauce from the pan.
6. Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds if desired.
