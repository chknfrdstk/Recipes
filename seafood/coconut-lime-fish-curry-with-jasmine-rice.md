# Coconut Lime Fish Curry with Jasmine Rice

## Ingredients

- 1 Ib white fish fillets (cod or tilapia), cut into chunks
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 carrot, chopped
- 1 (13.5 oz) can full-fat coconut milk
- 1/2 cup fish stock or water
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp red curry paste
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 cup jasmine rice

## Instructions

1. Cook jasmine rice according to package directions.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion and cook until softened.
3. Stir in garlic, red bell pepper, and carrot; cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add red curry paste and ginger; cook for 1 minute more.
5. Pour in coconut milk and fish stock. Bring to a simmer.
6. Gently add fish to the curry.nCook for 5-7 minutes, or until fish is cooked through.
7. Stir in lime juice and cilantro.
8. Season with salt and pepper.
9. Serve curry over jasmine rice.
