# Ceviche

## Ingredients

- 1 lb fish (cod, snapper, halibut) diced
- 6-8 limes juiced
- 1/2 red onion diced
- 1-2 jalapeños diced (seeds removed)
- 1-2 tomatoes diced
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro
- 1 orange juiced
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Begin by placing the diced fish in a bowl. Squeeze enough limes (6-8) to cover the fish, submerging it completely, then refrigerate for 30 minutes to 1 hour until the fish turns opaque and "cooks" in the lime juice.
2. While marinating, combine jalapeños, tomatoes, and fresh cilantro.
3. Once the fish is ready, drain most of the lime juice, leaving just enough to keep it moist. Add the chopped vegetables and cilantro, season with salt and pepper, and add the juice of 1-2 oranges for sweetness and extra flavor.
4. Let it sit for another 10-15 minutes to blend the flavors, then serve chilled with tortilla chips, tostadas, or as a taco filling.
