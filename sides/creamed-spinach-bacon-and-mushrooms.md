# Creamed Spinach, Bacon and Mushrooms

## Ingredients

- 4 slices of bacon chopped
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cups beef broth onion finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic minced
- 8 oz mushrooms sliced
- 10 oz fresh spinach chopped
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cups beef broth grated Parmesan cheese
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, cook the chopped bacon over medium heat until crispy. Remove and set aside leaving the bacon fat.
2. Add butter to the skillet. Once melted add the onions and garlic. Sauté until the onions are translucent.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook until they are browned and tender.
4. Add the chopped spinach and cook until wilted.
5. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a simmer.
6. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the Parmesan cheese, allowing it to melt into the sauce.
7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
8. Continue cooking the sauce until it has thickened to your liking.
9. Stir in bacon pieces and remove from the heat.
10. Serve as a warm side dish or over pasta, rice or your favorite protein.
