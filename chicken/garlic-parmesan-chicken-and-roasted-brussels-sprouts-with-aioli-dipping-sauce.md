# Garlic Parmesan Chicken and Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Aioli Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

### For the Brussels Sprouts

- 1 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

### For the Aioli Dipping Sauce

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine chicken with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, and Parmesan cheese.
2. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
3. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
4. Toss the Brussels sprouts with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper. Spread the Brussels sprouts in a single layer on a baking sheet.
5. Roast for 20-25 minutes, or until tender-crisp.
6. Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Grill the chicken for 2-3 minutes per side or until cooked through.
7. In a small bowl, whisk together mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
8. Arrange the grilled chicken and roasted Brussels sprouts on plates. Serve with the Aioli Dipping Sauce.

## Tips

- For a spicier sauce, add a pinch of red pepper flakes to the Aioli Dipping Sauce.
