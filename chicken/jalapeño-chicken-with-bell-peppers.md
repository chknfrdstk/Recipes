# Jalapeño Chicken with Bell Peppers

<img width="600" src="images/jalapeño-chicken-with-bell-peppers.jpg" alt="Jalapeño Chicken with Bell Peppers">

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup bell peppers, sliced (mix of red, yellow, and green)
- 1 small onion, sliced
- 2-3 jalapeños, sliced (adjust to your spice preference)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, heat the olive oil over medium heat.
2. Add the chicken pieces to the skillet. Season with salt and pepper. Cook until browned and cooked through.
3. Stir in the sliced bell peppers, onion, jalapeños, and garlic. Cook until the veggies are tender.
4. Sprinkle in the cumin and paprika. Mix everything well and cook for an additional minute.
5. Remove from heat and garnish with fresh cilantro.
6. Serve hot with rice, tortillas, or on its own.
