# One-Pan Coconut-Lime Chicken

<img width="600" src="images/one-pan-coconut-lime-chicken.jpg" alt="One-Pan Coconut-Lime Chicken">

## Ingredients

- 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- Juice and zest of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
- 2 tbsp fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
- 1 tbsp toasted coconut flakes (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Heat olive oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper. Once the oil is hot, add the chicken thighs and sear for 4-5 minutes on each side, or until golden brown.
4. Remove the chicken from the skillet and set aside.
5. In the same skillet, add garlic and ginger, cooking until fragrant.
6. Pour in the coconut milk, lime juice, lime zest, soy sauce, honey, and chili flakes (if using). Stir to combine.
7. Return the chicken to the skillet, spooning some of the coconut sauce over the chicken.
8. Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
9. Remove the skillet from the oven, garnish with fresh cilantro and toasted coconut flakes.
10. Serve the chicken with extra sauce from the pan.
