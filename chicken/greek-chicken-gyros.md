# Greek Chicken Gyros

<img width="600" src="images/greek-chicken-gyros.jpg" alt="Greek Chicken Gyros">

## Ingredients

### For the marinade

- 2 lbs chicken thighs, boneless skinless
- 6 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 lemons, zested and juice
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the tzatziki sauce

- 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 2 cucumbers, grated and squeezed to remove water
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- Salt to taste

### For the gyros

- 8 pita bread of flat breads
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cucumbers, sliced
- Lettuce, shredded
- Feta cheese

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine olive oil, yogurt, garlic, oregano, cumin, paprika, coriander, lemon zest, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
2. Add the chicken to the marinade and mix well to coat. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight for best flavor.
3. Mix yogurt, grated cucumber, garlic, olive oil, dill, and lemon juice in a bowl. Season with salt. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to let the flavors meld.
4. Heat a skillet or grill pan over medium-high heat.
5. Cook the chicken for 5-6 minutes per side, or until fully cooked and slightly charred.
6. Let it rest for 5 minutes before slicing into strips.
7. Warm the pita bread in a dry skillet or the oven.
8. Layer the pita bread with lettuce, chicken, tomato, onion, cucumber and tzatziki sauce. Add feta if desired.

## Tips

- For the most tender chicken, use thighs instead of breasts.
- Make the marinade and tzatziki a day ahead to save time.
- Squeeze out as much water as possible from the grated cucumber to prevent a watery tzatziki.
- Use a grill for a smoky flavor.
- Customize with toppings like olives or roasted red peppers.
