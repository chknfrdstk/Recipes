# One Pan Honey Garlic Chicken with Red Potatoes and Green Beans

<img width="600" src="images/one-pan-honey-garlic-chicken-with-red-potatoes-and-green-beans.jpg" alt="One Pan Honey Garlic Chicken with Red Potatoes and Green Beans">

## Ingredients

- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 pound red potatoes, halved
- 2 cups green beans, trimmed
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, olive oil, minced garlic, dried thyme, salt, and pepper. Add the chicken thighs to the bowl and coat them well with the marinade. Let them marinate for at least 15 minutes.
3. In a large baking dish, arrange the halved red potatoes in a single layer. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat.
4. Place the marinated chicken thighs on top of the potatoes, pouring any remaining marinade over the chicken.
5. Bake in the preheated oven for 20 minutes.
6. After 20 minutes, remove the baking dish from the oven and add the green beans around the chicken and potatoes. Toss the green beans in the marinade left in the dish.
7. Return the dish to the oven and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are tender.
8. Once done, remove from the oven and let it rest for a few minutes.
9. Garnish with fresh parsley before serving.
