# Melt in Your Mouth Chicken

## Ingredients

- 2 - 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp garlic powder

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
2. In a small bowl, add the mayonnaise, Parmesan cheese and the seasonings. Mix to combine.
3. Place the chicken on a baking dish and spray with non-stick cooking spray.
4. Spread the mayonnaise mixture on top of the chicken.
5. Bake the chicken unfortunately 45 minutes or until cooked through.
6. Serve warm.
