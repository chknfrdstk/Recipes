# One Pan Cheesy Jalapeño Chicken

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 tsp butter
- 1/2 cup onion chopped
- 2 jalapenos seeded and diced
- 1 tbsp garlic minced
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/3 cup chicken broth
- 2 oz cream cheese
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese divided
- Cilantro for garnish
- Sliced fresh or pickled jalapeno for garnish
- Green onion sliced for garnish

## Instructions

1. Combine the cumin, chili powder, garlic powder, salt and pepper, set aside.
2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
3. Sprinkle the chicken thighs with the spice mixture on each side.
4. Sear the chicken in the skillet 2-3 minutes on each side until nicely browned and cooked through, remove from skillet and set aside.
5. Add 1 tablespoon butter to the skillet to melt. Add the onion, jalapenos and garlic and sauté for 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Add the cream, broth, cream cheese and reduce heat to low.
7. Stir mixture until cream cheese melts completely and add 1/2 cup of the shredded cheese to the sauce, stir well.
8. Add the chicken to the skillet and cover with the remaining cheese.
9. Place a lid on the skillet and let simmer over low for 6-8 minutes.
10. Plate the chicken and garnish with cilantro and jalapeno slices.
