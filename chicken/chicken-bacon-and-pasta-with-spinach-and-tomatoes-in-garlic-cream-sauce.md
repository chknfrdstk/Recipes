# Chicken Bacon and Pasta with Spinach and Tomatoes in Garlic Cream Sauce

## Ingredients

- 8 oz pasta
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 6 strips of bacon cooked and crumbled
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes halved
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley chopped for garnish

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
2. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. In a large skillet, heat 1 tbsp of olive oil over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until browned and cooked through. Remove the chicken and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, add another tbsp of olive oil. Add the minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
4. Add the halved cherry tomatoes and cook until they start to soften.
5. Stir in the fresh spinach and cook until wilted.
6. Pour in the heavy cream and bring to a simmer. Add the grated Parmesan cheese and red pepper flakes. Stir until the cheese is melted and the sauce has thickened slightly.
7. Slice the cooked chicken into strips and add it back to the skillet along with the crumbled bacon.
8. Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss until well coated.
9. Serve the pasta garnished with fresh chopped parsley.
