# Cajun Chicken and Sausage Pasta Skillet

## Ingredients

- 12 oz penne pasta
- 1 lb chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 8 oz andouille or smoked sausage, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 small onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 cup parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Cook the penne pasta according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
2. In a large skillet over medium heat, add olive oil and sauté the sliced sausage until browned. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same skillet, melt the butter and add the chicken pieces.
4. Season with Cajun seasoning, paprika, and black pepper. Cook until the chicken is browned and cooked through. Remove and set aside with the sausage.
5. Add diced onion and bell pepper to the skillet. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until softened, then add the garlic and cook for another minute.
6. Pour in the chicken broth and heavy cream, stirring to combine.
7. Let it simmer for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens.
8. Lower the heat and stir in the parmesan cheese until melted and smooth.
9. Add the cooked pasta, chicken, and sausage back into the skillet.
10. Toss everything to coat evenly in the creamy Cajun sauce.
11. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
