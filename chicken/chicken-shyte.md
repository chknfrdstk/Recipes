# Chicken Shyte

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs, cubed
- 2 cups fresh broccoli florets
- 6 oz bacon, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 4 oz cream cheese
- 2 oz ranch seasoning
- 1 cup Colby jack cheese, shredded
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- Green onions, diced for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet, cook the bacon until the fat is rendered and bacon is crispy. Remove and set aside, leaving the grease.
2. Add the chicken to the skillet and brown.
3. Just before the chicken is done add the garlic and mushrooms, cook until fragrant.
4. When the chicken is cooked add the garlic fragrant, add the bacon back to the skillet.
5. Add the ranch seasoning.
6. Add the cream cheese and stir until well combined.
7. Add heavy whipping cream and broccoli florets. Cook until the broccoli reaches your desired consistency.
8. Stir in the shredded cheese and mix until combined and melted.
9. Garnish with the green onions and serve.
