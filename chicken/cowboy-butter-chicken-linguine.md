# Cowboy Butter Chicken Linguine

## Ingredients

- 8 oz linguine
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 5 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (adjust to heat preference)
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh chives
- 1 tbsp chopped fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
- Optional: 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Cook linguine according to package directions. Reserve 1/4 cup pasta water and drain.
2. Season sliced chicken with salt and pepper. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add chicken and cook until golden and cooked through, 5-7 minutes.
4. Remove from skillet and set aside.
5. In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
6. Stir in Dijon mustard, smoked paprika, cayenne, red pepper flakes, and lemon juice. Simmer for 2 minutes.
7. Stir in chopped herbs and return the chicken to the pan. Toss to coat.
8. Add the cooked linguine and toss everything together. Add a splash of reserved pasta water if needed to loosen the sauce.
9. Sprinkle with Parmesan (optional) and serve hot.
