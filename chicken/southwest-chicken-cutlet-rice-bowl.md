# Southwest Chicken Cutlet Rice Bowl

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken Cutlets

- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, pounded thin
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### For the Rice

- 1 cup white rice
- 1 3/4 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 tsp salt

### For the Salsa

- 1 tomato, diced
- 1/2 red onion, diced
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

### For the Creamy Sauce

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

## Instructions

1. Combine rice and chicken broth in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes, or until liquid is absorbed.
2. Season flour with salt and pepper.
3. Dip chicken breasts in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs.
4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
5. Cook chicken cutlets for 3-4 minutes per side, or until golden brown and cooked through.
6. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, sour cream, lime juice, garlic, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
7. Divide rice among bowls. Top with chicken cutlets, salsa, and creamy sauce.

## Tips

- For a spicier dish, add a pinch of red pepper flakes to the chicken seasoning.
- You can use different types of cheese, such as cheddar or Monterey Jack, for the creamy sauce.
- Serve with your favorite sides, such as avocado or tortilla chips.
