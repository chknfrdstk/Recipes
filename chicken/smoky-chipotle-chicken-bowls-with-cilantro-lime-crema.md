# Smoky Chipotle Chicken Bowls with Cilantro Lime Crema

## Ingredients

### For the Chicken

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into strips
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp chipotle peppers in adobo sauce
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Cilantro Lime Crema

- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/4 tsp chili powder (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Bowls

- 1 cup cooked rice or quinoa
- 1 cup chopped bell peppers
- 1/2 cup chopped red onion
- Toppings: avocado, salsa, cheese, etc. (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, combine olive oil, chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
2. Place the chicken strips in a shallow dish. Pour the marinade over the chicken and let it marinate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 2 hours.
3. Grill or pan-fry the chicken for 3-4 minutes per side, or until cooked through.
4. In a small bowl, combine sour cream, cilantro, lime juice, garlic, chili powder (if using), salt, and pepper. Mix well.
5. Divide the cooked rice or quinoa among bowls. Top with grilled chicken, bell peppers, red onion, and cilantro lime crema. Add your favorite toppings.

## Tips

- You can use any type of protein you like for this recipe.
- For a spicier marinade, add more chipotle peppers in adobo sauce.
