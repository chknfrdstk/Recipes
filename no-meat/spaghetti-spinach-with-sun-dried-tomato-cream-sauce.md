# Spaghetti & Spinach with Sun-Dried Tomato Cream Sauce

## Ingredients

- 5 oz baby spinach, coarsely chopped
- 8 oz thin spaghetti
- ½ cup slivered oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes + 1 tbsp oil from jar
- ½ cup halved, thinly sliced onion
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- ¼ tsp crushed red pepper
- ¼ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp ground pepper
- 1 cup low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
- ½ cup sour cream
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter

## Instructions

1. Place spinach in a large colander. Cook spaghetti, then drain over the spinach to wilt.
2. Heat oil in a skillet, sauté onions and sun-dried tomatoes for 3 minutes. Add garlic and spices; cook 1 minute.
3. Add broth, cook until reduced.
4. Stir in sour cream, Parmesan, and butter. Toss with pasta and spinach.
