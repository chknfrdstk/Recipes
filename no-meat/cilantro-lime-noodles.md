# Cilantro Lime Noodles

<img width="600" src="images/cilantro-lime-noodles.jpg" alt="Cilantro Lime Noodles">

## Ingredients

- 8 oz rice noodles (or your favorite noodles)
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- Juice of 2 limes
- Zest of 1 lime
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 tbsp honey or agave syrup
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional for a little heat)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Chopped green onions, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Cook the rice noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together lime juice, lime zest, soy sauce, honey (or agave), and red pepper flakes (if using). Set aside.
3. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté until fragrant.
4. Add the cooked noodles to the pan with the garlic. Toss them to coat in the olive oil.
5. Pour the cilantro lime sauce over the noodles and toss to combine, ensuring the noodles are evenly coated.
6. Stir in the fresh chopped cilantro and season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Serve the noodles in bowls, and garnish with extra cilantro and chopped green onions if desired.
