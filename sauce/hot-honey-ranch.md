# Hot Honey Ranch

<img width="600" src="images/hot-honey-ranch.jpg" alt="Hot Honey Ranch">

## Ingredients

- 1 cup avocado oil
- 1/2 cup crushed red chili flakes
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tsp salt
- 5 oz Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp fresh dill, roughly chopped
- 1/3 cup honey

### Homemade Ranch Seasoning

- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp dried parsley
- 1/4 tsp mustard powder
- Salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat avocado oil to 250°F.
2. To a wok or deep pan, add the crushed red chili flakes, sesame seeds, garlic and salt. Pour hot oil over the ingredients and mix.
3. Let the chili oil cool.
4. Once the chili oil has cooled, add all remaining ingredients: Greek yogurt, Ranch Seasoning, honey, and dill. Mix thoroughly.

## Notes

- Makes 1 1/2 cups.
- MIX WELL: Use a whisk to thoroughly blend everything together. The oil needs to be emulsified.
- STORAGE: Refrigerate for up to 10 days in an air-tight container.
- HEAT LEVEL: This is HOT HOT HOT. If you want to make a milder version, cut the chili flakes down to about 2 TBSP.
