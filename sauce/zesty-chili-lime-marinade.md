# Zesty Chili Lime Marinade

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds meat or veggies of choice
- ½ cup olive oil
- 2 limes, zested and juiced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp agave nectar (or honey)
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 ½ tsp ground cumin
- 1 ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper

## Instructions

1. Add the olive oil, lime zest, lime juice, garlic, agave, ancho chile powder, ground cumin, smoked paprika, dried oregano, kosher salt and ground black pepper to a medium bowl or jar.
2. Whisk or shake to combine. Set aside, or store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 5 days.
3. Pour the chili lime marinade over your meat or veggies of choice. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for 6-8 hours.
4. Air fry, bake or grill according to meat selection
