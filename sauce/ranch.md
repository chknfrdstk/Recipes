# Ranch

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- 3/4 tsp dried parsley
