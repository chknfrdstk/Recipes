# French Onion Beef and Noodles

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb beef stew meat
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 can French onion soup
- 3 cups beef broth
- 12 oz egg noodles
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese grated
- 1 cup French fried onions

## Instructions

1. On a large pot, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the stew meat and season with onions, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Sauté until the beef is browned on all sides.
2. Pour in the French onion soup and beef broth. Simmer for 10 minutes.
3. Add the egg noodles and cook until done.
4. Remove the pot from the heat. Stir in the sour cream and Parmesan cheese until well combined.
5. Serve warm garnished with fried onions.
