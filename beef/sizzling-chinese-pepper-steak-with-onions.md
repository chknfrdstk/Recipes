# Sizzling Chinese Pepper Steak with Onions

## Ingredients

- 1 lb flank steak thinly sliced against the grain
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large onion thinly sliced
- 2 bell peppers (any color) thinly sliced
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1 tbsp ginger minced
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup beef broth
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- Cooked rice
- Green onions chopped for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the sliced flank steak, 2 tbsp soy sauce and corn starch. mix weak and marinate for 15 minutes.
2. In a large skillet or wok, over medium-high heat, stir-fry the marinated beef until cooked through. Remove and set aside.
3. In the same skillet stir-fry the onions and bell peppers for 3 - 4 minutes or until tender crisp. Add the garlic and ginger, stir-fry until fragrant.
4. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, beef broth, oyster sauce, hoisin sauce, sugar black pepper and red pepper flakes.
5. Pour the sauce mixture over the beef and vegetables. Stir and cook for 2 - 3 minutes until the sauce thickens and heats through.
6. Serve over cooked rice and garnish with chopped green onions.
