# Cowboy Stew

<img width="600" src="images/cowboy-stew.jpg" alt="Cowboy Stew">

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 lb smoked sausage sliced into rounds
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can Rotel tomatoes
- 1 can pinto beans drained and rinsed
- 1 can corn drained
- 4 medium potatoes
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 4 cups beef broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup cilantro for garnish
- Sliced jalapeño for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, brown hamburger meat until cooked through. Remove and set aside.
2. Add sliced sausage to the pot and sauté until browned.
3. Add hamburger meat back to the pot.
4. Add taco seasoning and chili powder and stir until evenly combined.
5. Add tomatoes, beans, corn, potatoes and beef broth. Stir and cook until the potatoes are done.
6. Salt and pepper to taste.
