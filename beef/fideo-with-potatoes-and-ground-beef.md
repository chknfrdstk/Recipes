# Fideo with Potatoes and Ground Beef

<img width="600" src="images/fideo-with-potatoes-and-ground-beef.jpg" alt="Fideo with Potatoes and Ground Beef">

## Ingredients

- 8 oz fideo noodles
- 1 lb ground beef
- 2 medium yellow potatoes, peeled and chopped
- 1 medium sweet onion, diced
- 2 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Chop the potatoes into bite-sized pieces. Dice the onion and mince the garlic.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until translucent, then add the garlic and cook for about 1 minute, until fragrant.
3. Add the ground beef and brown, crumbling it as it cooks.
4. Stir in the potatoes and tomato sauce and cook for a few minutes.
5. Pour in the chicken broth, covering everything, and bring to a simmer.
6. Stir in the fideo noodles and cook for 8-10 minutes, until tender but still al dente.
7. Taste and adjust the salt as needed. Serve topped with cilantro or parsley, if using.
