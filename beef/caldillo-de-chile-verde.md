# Caldillo de Chile Verde

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1.5 lbs beef stew meat cut into inch cubes
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 large onion chopped
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1 lb tomatillos husked and chopped
- 3 -4 green chiles Anaheim or poblanos roasted peeled and chopped
- 1 jalapeño seeded and chopped
- 4 cups beef broth
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- Fresh cilantro chopped (for garnish)
- Lime wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Season the meat with salt and pepper. Add the meat to the pot in batches, browning all sides. Transfer the meat to a plate.
2. In the same pot, add the onion and cook until softened. Add the garlic cooking until fragrant.
3. Stir in the chopped tomatillos, green chiles and jalapeño. Cook until the tomatillos start to break down.
4. Return the browned beef to the pot. Add the beef broth, ground cumin and dried oregano. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Simmer, partially covered, for 1.5 to 2 hrs, or until the beef is very tender.
5. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and pepper if needed. The soup should be somewhat thick but still brothy.
6. Ladle the Caldillo de Chile Verde into bowls. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve with lime wedges on the side.
