# Creamy Spicy Jalapeño Shrimp Soup

<img width="600" src="images/creamy-spicy-jalapeno-shrimp-soup.jpg" alt="Creamy Spicy Jalapeño Shrimp Soup">

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 jalapeños, diced (seeds removed for less heat)
- 1 can (14 oz) coconut milk
- 2 cups seafood broth (if you can find it) chicken or vegetable broth can be substituted
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Optional garnish: fresh cilantro, lime wedges, or sliced jalapeños

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat 1 tablespoon of oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for 4-5 minutes until softened. Stir in the minced garlic and diced jalapeños, and cook until fragrant.
2. Pour in the broth, coconut milk, ground cumin, and smoked paprika.
3. Stir to combine and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer.
4. Add the shrimp to the pot and simmer until the shrimp are pink and fully cooked.
5. Season the soup with salt and pepper to taste. Adjust the heat by adding more jalapeños or a dash of cayenne pepper if desired.
6. Ladle the soup into bowls and garnish with fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime inice or sliced jalapeños for an extra kick.
