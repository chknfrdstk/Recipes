# Crock Pot Green Enchilada Chicken Soup

## Ingredients

- 3 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 28 oz can green enchilada sauce
- 32 oz chicken broth
- 1 medium onion diced
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 cups Monterey Jack cheese
- 4 oz cream cheese cube at room temp
- 8 oz Herdez guacamole salsa
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Garnishes

- Cilantro
- Green onion
- Avocado
- Fried tortilla strips

## Instructions

1. Season chicken thighs with salt and pepper.
2. In a skillet, over medium-high heat add olive oil brown chicken thighs. Remove and add to the crock pot.
3. In the same skillet, add died onions and sauté until translucent. Add to the crock pot.
4. To the crock pot add enchilada sauce and chicken broth. Cook on low for 6-8 hours or on high for 3-4 hours.
5. About 30 minutes before serving, remove and shred the chicken. Add the shredded chicken back to the crock pot.
6. Add the jack cheese, cream cheese, heavy whipping cream and guacamole salsa to the crock pot. Stirring until the cream cheese is melted and incorporated fully.
7. Ladle into bowls and serve garnished with cilantro, green onions, avocado and tortilla strips.
