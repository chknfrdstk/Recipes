# Chicken Avocado Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 1/4 cups chopped green onions including whites
- 2 jalapeños seeded and minced
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 4 (14.5 oz) cans low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 Roma tomatoes seeded and diced
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1/3 cup chopped cilantro
- 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 3 medium avocados diced
- Fried tortilla strips, monterrey jack cheese, queso fresco cheese, sour cream for serving garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pot heat 1 Tbsp olive oil over medium heat. Once hot, add green onions and jalapenos and saute until tender, about 2 minutes, adding garlic during last 30 seconds of sauteing.
2. Add chicken broth, tomatoes, cumin, season with salt and pepper to taste and add chicken thighs. Bring mixture to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat to medium, cover with lid and allow to cook, stirring occasionally, until chicken has cooked through.
3. Reduce to warm heat, remove chicken from pan and let rest on a cutting board 5 minutes, then shred chicken and return to soup. Stir in cilantro, and lime juice.
4. Serve garnished with tortilla strips, diced avocado, cilatro, queso fresco cheese, Monterey Jack cheese and sour cream.
