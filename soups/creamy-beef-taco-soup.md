# Creamy Beef Taco Soup

<img width="600" src="images/creamy-beef-taco-soup.jpg" alt="Creamy Beef Taco Soup">

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 large onion chopped
- 2 cloves garlic minced
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 2 cans pinto beans drained and rinsed
- 1 can corn drained
- 1 can Rotel tomatoes
- 4 cups beef broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup shredded Mexican blend cheese
- 1 cup sour cream
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Toppings

- Fresh cilantro
- Tortilla strips
- Sliced avocado
- Shredded Mexican cheese blend
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks. Drain any excess fat.
2. Add the onion and cook until translucent. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant.
3. Stir in the taco seasoning and mix until everything is well coated. Add the pinto beans, corn and diced tomatoes. Stir to combine.
4. Pour in the beef broth and bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and shredded cheese until the cheese is melted and the soup is creamy. Add the sour cream and mix well. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Ladle the soup into bowls and add your desired toppings.
