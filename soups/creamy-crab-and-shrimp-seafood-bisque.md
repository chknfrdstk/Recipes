# Creamy Crab and Shrimp Seafood Bisque

## Ingredients

### For the Soup Base

- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups seafood stock
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp Old Bay seasoning
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Seafood

- 1/2 lb cooked shrimp, peeled, deveined, and chopped
- 1/2 lb cooked crab meat, shredded

### For the Garnish

- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Fresh parsley, chopped (optional)
- Crusty bread or oyster crackers

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the butter and olive oil over medium heat.
2. Add the diced onion and sauté until softened.
3. Add the minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
4. Stir in the tomato paste, seafood stock, Old Bay seasoning, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
5. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer.
6. Stir in the milk and heavy cream, allowing the soup to gently simmer for 5 minutes.
7. Add the cooked shrimp and crab meat to the pot.
8. Simmer for an additional 5-7 minutes, letting the flavors meld together.
9. Ladle the bisque into bowls.
10. Garnish each serving with grated Parmesan cheese, fresh parsley, and a side of crusty bread or oyster crackers.
