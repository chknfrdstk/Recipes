# Sausage Tortellini Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 lb Italian sausage
- 1 small onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 1 package refrigerated cheese tortellini
- 2 cups fresh spinach

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, cook the Italian sausage over medium heat until browned. Add the diced onion and garlic, and cook until the onion is translucent.
2. Stir in the chicken broth, diced tomatoes, basil, salt, and pepper. Bring the soup to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 10 minutes.
3. Add the tortellini and fresh spinach to the pot, and cook for another 5-7 minutes until the tortellini is cooked through.
4. Serve hot and enjoy!
