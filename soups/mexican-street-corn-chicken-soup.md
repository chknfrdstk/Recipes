# Mexican Street Corn Chicken Soup

<img width="600" src="images/mexican-street-corn-chicken-soup.jpg" alt="Mexican Street Corn Chicken Soup">

## Ingredients

- 4 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs cubed
- 1-2 yellow onions depending on size medium diced
- 2 jalapenos seeded and diced
- 8 cups chicken broth (2 32oz boxes)
- 2 cups sour cream
- 1 cup shredded monerey jack cheese (more if you want to add as a topping when serving)
- 8 cloves minced garlic
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 2 tbsp chicken bouillon
- 2 tbsp cumin
- 4 cups frozen corn
- 1 stick of butter
- 1 cup fresh cilantro
- olive oil
- cojita cheese
- fried tortilla strips
- sliced avocado
- 1 bunch green onions sliced. Seperate whites and greens.

## Instructions

1. In a skillet melt butter and roast corn until slightly chared. Set aside.
2. In a large soup pot heat olive oil. Add in onion, green onion whites, jalapeno and saute until softened.
3. Add garlic. oregano, cumin, chili powder and chicken bouillon. Stir to combine.
4. Add broth, cubed chicken thighs and pepper to taste. Do not add salt. Salt will come from the chicken bouillon.
5. Bring to a boil.
6. Reduce heat and simmer until the chicken is cooked through.
7. Add sour cream, monterey jack cheese, cilantro, roasted corn and lime juice.
8. Serve with cojita cheese, fried tortilla strips, avocado, green onions and more shreaded cheese if desired.
