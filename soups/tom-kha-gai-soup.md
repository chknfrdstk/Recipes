# Tom Kha Gai Soup

<img width="600" src="images/tom-kha-gai-soup.jpg" alt="Tom Kha Gai Soup">

## Ingredients

- 3 cups chicken broth
- 1 can (13.5 oz) coconut milk
- Lime wedges
- 2 stalks lemongrass: Smashed and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 4 slices fresh galangal: A key ingredient; ginger can be used as a substitute if unavailable.
- 5 kaffir lime leaves: Torn into pieces
- 2-3 Thai chilies: Adjust according to your spice tolerance.
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs thinly sliced
- 1 cup mushrooms button or shiitake mushrooms work best.
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 2 tablespoons lime juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar optional
- Fresh cilantro chopped for garnish.

## Instructions

1. In a large pot over medium heat and pour in the chicken broth and coconut milk
2. Add the lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaves, and Thai chilies to the pot.
3. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer and allow it to cook for about 10 minutes.
4. Add the thinly sliced chicken to the simmering broth. Stir gently to separate the slices.
5. Add the sliced mushrooms to the pot.
6. Simmer the soup for 5-7 minutes, or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
7. Stir in the fish sauce and lime juice, then add the sugar if using.
8. Taste the soup and adjust the seasoning to balance the salty, tangy, and sweet flavors to your liking.
9. Ladle the soup into bowls.
10. Garnish each serving with fresh cilantro.
11. Serve with lime wedges on the side for an additional burst of fresh flavor.

## Serving Suggestions

Tom Kha Gai pairs beautifully with steamed jasmine rice or rice noodles for a more filling meal. Serve it alongside other Thai favorites like spring rolls or papaya salad for a complete dining experience.
