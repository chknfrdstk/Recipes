# Potsticker Soup with Mushrooms & Bok Choy

## Ingredients

- 1 package of frozen potstickers
- 4 cups chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms
- 2 cups bok choy, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 inch piece of ginger, grated
- 2 green onions, sliced (reserve some for garnish)
- 2 tbsp soy sauce

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the chicken or vegetable broth over medium heat.
2. Add the garlic, grated ginger, and soy sauce to the pot. Let it simmer for 5 minutes to infuse the flavors.
3. Add the sliced mushrooms to the simmering broth. Cook for 3-5 minutes, or until they start to soften.
4. Gently place the frozen potstickers into the broth. Allow them to cook according to the package instructions, usually about 5-7 minutes, or until they float and are fully heated through.
5. Stir in the chopped bok choy and cook for an additional 2-3 minutes, or until the bok choy is tender but still bright green.
6. Taste the soup and add more soy sauce if needed for flavor. You can also add a pinch of salt or a dash of sesame oil for extra depth.
7. Ladle the soup into bowls and garnish with the sliced green onions.
8. Add a sprinkle of chili flakes or a drizzle of chili oil for a spicy kick, if desired.
