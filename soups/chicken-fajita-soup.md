# Chicken Fajita Soup

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 bell pepper (any color), sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 cups chicken broth (low-sodium)
- 1 can (14.5 ounces) diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup sliced mushrooms
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, for garnish
- Lime wedges, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sliced bell pepper, cooking until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, chili powder, and smoked paprika. Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
3. Add the diced chicken to the pot and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Pour in the chicken broth and diced tomatoes. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes.
5. Add the sliced mushrooms and heavy cream to the pot. Stir well and simmer for another 5 minutes until the mushrooms are tender.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro and lime wedges.
