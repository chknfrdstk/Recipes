# Spicy Lemon Fish Soup

## Ingredients

### For the Soup Base

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red chili pepper, finely sliced (adjust to taste)
- 4 cups fish or chicken broth
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes, with juices
- 1 cup water (optional, for a thinner broth)
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional for extra heat)
- Salt and pepper to taste

### For the Fish and Veggies

- 1 lb white fish fillets, cut into bite-sized pieces (e.g., cod, tilapia, or halibut)
- 1 cup diced carrots
- 1 cup diced celery
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley or cilantro

### For the Lemon Flavor

- Juice of 2 lemons (about 1/4 cup)
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.
3. Stir in the garlic and chili pepper. Cook until fragrant.
4. Add the fish or chicken broth, diced tomatoes (with juices), water (if using), smoked paprika, ground cumin, and red pepper flakes.
5. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
6. Bring the soup to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer.
7. Add the carrots and celery to the pot.
8. Simmer for 10 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
9. Gently stir in the fish pieces. Simmer for 5-7 minutes, or until the fish is opaque and flakes easily with a fork
10. Stir in the lemon juice and fresh parsley and cilantro.
11. Taste the soup and adjust seasoning, adding more salt, pepper or lemon juice as needed.
12. Ladle the soup into bowls.
13. Garnish with thin lemon slices and extra herbs for a bright, fresh touch.
