# Mexican Corn Chicken Soup

<img width="600" src="images/mexican-corn-chicken-soup.jpg" alt="Mexican Corn Chicken Soup">

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion diced
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 1 can (15 oz) corn drained
- 1 can (15 oz) black beans drained and rinsed
- 1 can Rotel tomatoes
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro chopped for garnish
- Fried tortilla strips for garnish
- Queso fresco
- Lime wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add the chicken thighs and sear on each side for 2-3 minutes, until browned. Remove the chicken from the pot and set aside.
2. In the same pot, add the diced onion and garlic. Sauté for 3-4 minutes, until softened and fragrant.
3. Stir in the cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, cayenne (if using), salt, and pepper. Pour in the chicken broth, diced tomatoes, corn, and black beans. Return the chicken to the pot.
4. Bring the soup to a simmer and let it cook for 20-25 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Remove the chicken from the pot, shred it with two forks, and return it to the soup. Stir to combine.
6. Ladle the soup into bowls and garnish with fried tortilla strips, queso fresco and fresh cilantro. Serve with lime wedges on the side.
