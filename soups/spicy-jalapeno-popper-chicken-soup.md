# Spicy Jalapeño Popper Chicken Soup

<img width="600" src="images/spicy-jalapeño-popper-chicken-soup.jpg" alt="Spicy Jalapeño Popper Chicken Soup">

## Ingredients

- 2.5 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs diced
- 1 pack of hickory smoked bacon diced
- 1 large onion diced
- 6 cloves garlic minced
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups fresh jalapeños diced
- 2 pkg cream cheese softened
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 can corn
- Chopped cilantro, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, cook the diced bacon until almost done.
2. Add the diced onion and diced jalapeño and cook until softened.
3. Add the minced garlic and cook for an additional minute until fragrant.
4. Add the diced chicken to the pot and cook until browned and no longer pink.
5. Pour in the chicken broth, chili powder, salt, and black pepper. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 15 minutes to blend the flavors.
6. Stir in the softened cream cheese and shredded cheddar cheese, mixing until melted and creamy.
7. Add the corn and cook for an additional 5 minutes, until everything is heated through.
8. Ladle the soup into bowls and garnish with chopped cilantro if desired.
