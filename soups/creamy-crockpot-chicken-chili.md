# Creamy Crockpot Chicken Chili

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 1 can pinto beans drained and rinsed
- 1 can corn drained
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chilies
- 1 small onion chopped
- 1 packet ranch seasoning
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 8 oz cream cheese cubed
- 1/2 cup chicken broth

### Toppings

- Shredded cheddar cheese
- Cilantro
- Sliced jalapeños
- Sour cream
- Tortilla strips

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken at the bottom of the crockpot. Add the pinto beans, corn, diced tomatoes and chopped onion on top of the chicken.
2. Sprinkle in the ranch seasoning, chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder and paprika.
3. Pour in the chicken broth and stir to combine.
4. Place the cream cheese on top.
5. Cover and cook on low for 6-8 hours or high for 3-4 hours, until the chicken is cooked through and easily shredded.
6. Shred the chicken with 2 forks and stir until everything is well combined and the chili is creamy.
7. Serve hot with your desired toppings.
