# Thai Red Curry Chicken Noodle Soup

<img width="600" src="images/thai-red-curry-chicken-noodle-soup.jpg" alt="Thai Red Curry Chicken Noodle Soup">

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 lbs chicken thighs cut into 1 inch cubes
- 4 cloves garlic minced
- 1/2 onion diced
- 1 red bell pepper seeded and diced
- 1 zucchini cut into 1/4 moon slices
- 3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 2 tsp fresh ginger minced or grated
- 6 cups chicken broth
- 12 oz rice noodle of choice
- 1 can coconut milk
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- Cilantro for garnish
- Sliced green onions
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the chicken, salt and pepper. Sauté until the chicken is browned and mostly cooked through. Remove and west aside.
2. Add the garlic, onion, bell pepper and zucchini and sauté for 2-3 minutes. Add the red curry paste and ginger. Sauté until fragrant.
3. Add the chicken back to the pot.
4. Add the broth and rice noodles to the pot and bring to a boil, then reduce heat to medium until the noodles are al dente.
5. Add the coconut milk, soy sauce and lime juice. Stir until warmed through. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
6. Serve warm garnished with cilantro and sliced green onions.
