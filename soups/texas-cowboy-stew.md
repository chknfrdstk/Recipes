# Texas Cowboy Stew

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground beef
- 1 lb smoked sausage sliced
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 can diced tomatoes with green chilis
- 1 can pinto beans drained and rinsed
- 1 can corn drained
- 4 medium potatoes peeled and diced
- 1 packet taco seasoning
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 4 cups beef broth
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro for garnish
- Sliced jalapeño for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pot or Dutch oven, cook the ground beef over medium heat, breaking it up as you do. Add the sliced sausage and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
2. Add the diced tomatoes (with jucies), diced tomatoes with green chilis, pinto beans, corn and diced potatoes. Sprinkle in the taco seasoning and chili powder. Pour in the beef broth. Stir until well combined. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Bring the stew to a rolling boil, then reduce heat and simmer for 25-30 minutes.
4. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed.
5. Ladle into bowls and garnish with cilantro and jalapeños.
