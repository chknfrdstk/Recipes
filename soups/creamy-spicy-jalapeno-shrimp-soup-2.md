# Creamy Spicy Jalapeño Shrimp Soup

<img width="600" src="images/creamy-spicy-jalapeno-shrimp-soup-2.jpg" alt="Creamy Spicy Jalapeño Shrimp Soup">

## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 jalapeños, seeded and chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, for garnish

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the diced onion and sauté until translucent.
2. Stir in the minced garlic and chopped jalapeños, cooking until fragrant.
3. Sprinkle in the ground cumin and smoked paprika, stirring to combine. Pour in the vegetable broth and bring the mixture to a simmer.
4. Add the shrimp and corn to the pot, cooking until the shrimp turn pink and are cooked through.
5. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the heavy cream, allowing the soup to warm through.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Serve the soup hot, garnished with fresh cilantro for an added burst of flavor.
