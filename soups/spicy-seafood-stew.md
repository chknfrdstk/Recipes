# Spicy Seafood Stew

## Ingredients

- 1 lb mixed seafood (shrimp, scallops, and squid)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cups fish broth (no alcohol)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
- Juice of 1 lemon

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add onion, garlic, and bell pepper, sauteing until the onion becomes translucent.
3. Stir in diced tomatoes, fish broth, tomato paste, red pepper flakes, smoked paprika, and thyme. Bring to a simmer.
4. Add the mixed seafood to the pot.
5. Simmer gently for 5-7 minutes or until the seafood is cooked through.
6. Season with salt and black pepper.
7. Stir in lemon juice and fresh parsley just before serving.
8. Serve hot, ideally with crusty bread or over a bed of rice.
