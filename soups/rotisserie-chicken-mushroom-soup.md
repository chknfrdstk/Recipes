# Rotisserie Chicken Mushroom Soup

<img width="600" src="images/rotisserie-chicken-mushroom-soup.jpg" alt="Rotisserie Chicken Mushroom Soup">

## Ingredients

- 2-3 stalks celery, chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 4 cups chicken broth/stock
- 1 cup heavy cream/whipping cream
- 2 cups rotisserie chicken, shredded (or any cooked chicken)
- 2 cups spinach (fresh or frozen, no need to thaw)
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1-2 tbsp lemon juice (to taste) optional
- 1 medium white onion, diced (or substitute with red onion or shallots)
- 8 oz mushrooms (chestnut/ portabellini mushrooms erecommended, but any variety works)
- 1 tsp fresh thyme (or 1/2 tsp dried thyme)

## Instructions

1. Start by heating a splash of oil or a knob of butter in a larde pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the diced onion and chopped celery, cooking for about 5 minutes until they become soft and translucent.
2. Add the 8 oz of mushrooms to the pot. Saute the mushrooms for about 5 more minutes, allowing them to release their moisture and turn golden brown.
3. Stir in 1 tsp of fresh thyme (or 1/2 tsp dried thyme) and 3 minced garlic cloves. Cook for another minute until the garlic becomes fragrant.
4. Pour in 4 cups of chicken broth and 1 cup of heavy cream. Stir to combine and bring the mixture to a gentle simmer. Let it cook for about 5 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
5. Add chicken and spinach. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes.
6. Season the soup with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.
7. Serve the Rotisserie Chicken Mushroom Soup hot, with your favorite crusty bread on the side for dipping.
